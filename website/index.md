# Fuel Smarter on Every Trip

PathPay helps Seoul drivers pick the fueling stop that minimizes **total trip
cost**—fuel price, detour fuel, and time value—between Gwangjin and Songpa.

- 💻 **Code** – Browse and contribute on

- 💬 **Community** – Join the discussion, Slack, and mailing lists on the
  [Community page]({{ '/community' | relative_url }})

## Why PathPay

1. **Total-cost recommendations** instead of price-only rankings.
2. **Kakao-powered routing** ensures realistic detour distance and time.
3. **Actionable outputs** including top 3 stations and Kakao Map deeplinks.
4. **Optional visuals** with Folium maps for presentations or dashboards.
